# ICP: who we target

Read by `build-list`, `find-people` and `qualify-leads`. Edit it when the target changes.

## The offer
Mavan is running a confidential acquisition search. This list exists so the deal team can reach two kinds of people: owners who might sell, and the advisers who bring companies to market. Nothing in this kit writes to them.

## Group A: owner operators of US consumer and subscription companies
- **Industry:** consumer products and subscription businesses sold direct to consumers. Supplements, food and drink, beauty and personal care, pet, apparel, membership boxes, subscription services.
- **Size:** roughly 10 to 200 employees. Founder led or closely held.
- **Geography:** United States headquarters.
- **Default target:** founder, co founder, CEO, owner, president. The person who can say yes to a sale.
- **Go to someone else when:** the founder has left. Then the CEO, if the company is still independent.
- **Contacts per company:** one.

## Group B: sell side M&A bankers and brokers, lower middle market
- **Industry:** investment banks, M&A advisory boutiques and business brokerages that represent sellers in deals under about 100 million dollars of enterprise value.
- **Size:** 2 to 100 people. Boutiques, not bulge bracket.
- **Geography:** United States.
- **Default target:** managing director, partner, principal, founder. The person who runs sell side mandates.
- **Go to someone else when:** the firm has a named consumer practice lead. Then that person.
- **Contacts per company:** one. Two only when the firm has both a consumer lead and a managing partner.

## Hard exclusions: never contact
- Companies owned by a private equity sponsor or a strategic parent (Group A).
- Companies above about 300 employees (Group A).
- Buy side only advisers, deal sourcing services, lenders, debt advisers, ESOP specialists (Group B).
- Private equity funds, family offices, search funds, credit funds. They are buyers, not sellers' advisers.
- Recruiters, associations, listing platforms, marketplaces.
- Anyone outside the United States.
- Anyone Mavan has already spoken to. Keep that list in `leads/suppression.csv`.

## Signals worth researching
- A founder saying publicly they are considering a sale or moving to a chairman seat.
- A banker announcing a closed consumer deal in the last twelve months.
- Revenue or headcount plateau after years of growth.

## Signals to ignore
- Funding rounds. Venture backed companies are rarely sellers to a private buyer.
- Product launches and awards.
